HOW TO READ THE SIGNALS

How to interpret a signal

Velocity, governorate diversity, fan-in, fan-out and similar amounts use the preceding 30 minutes, including the current payment. Shared devices and circular transfers use the complete batch. Graph findings are retrospective.

Scenario labels are fictional ground truth. Metrics calculated from those labels describe only this batch. Imported transactions can optionally include expected_fraud; unlabeled imports do not show precision or recall.
