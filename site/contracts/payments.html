Terms of Use

High level description of the dataset including purpose, usage guidelines, and limitations

Purpose
One normalized record per payment, keyed by transaction_id within a batch.
Usage
Local analyst triage, linked-account investigation and labeled batch evaluation.
Limitations
This contract covers normalized payment records, not the hosted alert API, analyst verdicts or workspace envelopes. Conformance does not establish that a payment is legitimate or that a fraud signal is accurate.
